# ConAgra Foods (conagra-foods)

ConAgra Foods, Inc. was the prior corporate name of Conagra Brands. In 2015 the company spun off its foodservice ingredients business as Lamb Weston and renamed the remaining packaged-foods business to Conagra Brands, Inc. This profile is preserved as a historical alias; the active company profile lives at the [conagra-brands](https://github.com/api-evangelist/conagra-brands) repository. No public developer APIs were ever published under the ConAgra Foods brand.
